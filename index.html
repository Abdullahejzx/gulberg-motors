Inquiries

Frequently answered

Can I drive to Islamabad or the Northern areas?

Yes. Every SUV is cleared for the M-2 and M-3 motorways with e-tag toll clearance pre-installed. For Murree or Bhurban, all 4WD systems pass an altitude check before handover.

What is included with chauffeur service?

Standard shifts run 12 continuous hours with a uniformed, bilingual chauffeur trained in protocol security. Fuel packages and overtime are calculated transparently.

How quickly can a car be delivered in Gulberg?

Average delivery to Gulberg, Cavalry Ground, Cantt and Main Boulevard is under 60 minutes. DHA Phases 1–8 are typically serviced within 90 to 120 minutes.

Do you offer security escorts (VIP protocol)?

Yes. Vetted protocol guards and escort chase vehicles can be coordinated with 12 hours’ advance clearance.
